About this dataset
  • Symbol: ES (E-mini S&P 500 futures) — continuous front-month series.
  • Roll handling: ratio-adjusted across contract rolls. Levels won't match raw exchange prices on any single contract — suitable for returns and TA, not for absolute price targets or option-strike alignment.
  • Session definition: CME ETH trading day, Sun 17:00 CT → Fri 16:00 CT. Evening sessions roll into the next day's bar.
  • Source: ES_full_1min_continuous_ratio_adjusted, resampled from 1-minute → 60-minute → daily.
